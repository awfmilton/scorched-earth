Framed art tile for anything with a picture — ordnance, vehicles, structures, crafting materials.

```jsx
<IconTile sprite="void-bomb" tier={4} glow="violet" />
<IconTile sprite="clockwork-tank" size={56} fit="contain" src="assets/sprites/clockwork-tank.png" />
```

**Art pending.** The brand material contains three large composite concept sheets, not per-sprite files, so with no `src` the tile renders a tier-framed placeholder showing the sprite key's initials. That is deliberate — do not fill the gap with hand-drawn SVG or emoji. `SPRITE_KEYS` is the agreed naming manifest for the art still to be delivered.
